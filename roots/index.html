Word Roots

Most terms in organic chemistry are built from a few Greek and Latin parts. Here they are sorted by what they describe in chemistry, and each section says which kinds of Exam 2 question its roots help with.

Root meanings follow standard dictionary etymologies. Where an origin is disputed or unknown, the root says so. The example terms come from the course material: the lecture notes, the readings and the Fall 2024 exam.
